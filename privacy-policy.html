Privacy Policy

How CakeSquared handles information in our apps and on cakesquared.co.uk.

Last updated: September 2026

CakeSquared ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications and the website https://www.cakesquared.co.uk.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our apps and website.

Information We Collect

Our published App Store apps are configured such that we do not collect personal data from them.

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information such as names, email addresses, location data, contacts, photos, files, identifiers, or usage analytics from the apps, unless you voluntarily contact us for support.

In-App Purchases

If an app offers in-app purchases, payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store payment information.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may rely on Apple platform features necessary for operation (for example Apple Music control where an app offers it). Those services operate under Apple’s own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to third parties.

Children’s Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data from the apps, there is generally no personal information to access, modify, or delete. If you contact us directly, you may request deletion of any correspondence at any time.
