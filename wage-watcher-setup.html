Two devices.
One quick setup.

Begin on iPhone, then make sure the companion app is installed on the Apple Watch paired with it.

On iPhone

Start here
  1. 1
    Open the App Store

    Tap the blue App Store icon on your iPhone.

  2. 2
    Find Wage Watcher

    Search for Wage Watcher, choose the result, then tap Get.

  3. 3
    Open and add your pay

    Tap Open, enter your hourly rate, then choose when your working day starts and ends.

View Wage Watcher on the App Store

On Apple Watch

Then do this
  1. 1
    Open the Watch app on iPhone

    Tap My Watch, then scroll down to Available Apps.

  2. 2
    Install Wage Watcher

    Find Wage Watcher and tap Install. Keep both devices nearby while it loads.

  3. 3
    Open it from your watch

    Press the Digital Crown, find the Wage Watcher icon, then tap it.

    Your settings sync automatically

    Your hourly rate and working-day settings are sent from your iPhone to Apple Watch. Keep both devices nearby and connected while the first sync completes.

Can’t see it? Open the App Store on Apple Watch, search for Wage Watcher, then tap Get. Also check that your iPhone and watch are paired and connected.
