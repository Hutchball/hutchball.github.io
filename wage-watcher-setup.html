Set up WageWatcher on iPhone and Apple Watch

Install WageWatcher on your iPhone, add it to Apple Watch, and start seeing your earnings at a glance.

Two devices.
One quick setup.

Begin on iPhone, then make sure the companion app is installed on the Apple Watch paired with it.

On iPhone

Start here
  1. 1
    Open the App Store

    Tap the blue App Store icon on your iPhone.

  2. 2
    Find WageWatcher

    Search for WageWatcher, choose the result, then tap Get.

  3. 3
    Open and add your pay

    Tap Open, enter your hourly rate or annual salary, then choose your working days, hours and unpaid breaks.

View WageWatcher on the App Store

On Apple Watch

Then do this
  1. 1
    Open the Watch app on iPhone

    Tap My Watch, then scroll down to Available Apps.

  2. 2
    Install WageWatcher

    Find WageWatcher and tap Install. Keep both devices nearby while it loads.

  3. 3
    Open it from your watch

    Press the Digital Crown, find the WageWatcher icon, then tap it.

    Your settings sync automatically

    Your hourly rate and working-day settings are sent from your iPhone to Apple Watch. Keep both devices nearby and connected while the first sync completes.

Can’t see it? Open the App Store on Apple Watch, search for WageWatcher, then tap Get. Also check that your iPhone and watch are paired and connected.
